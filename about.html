The Organisation

About Kairon Technology

Founded on the principle that true security demands the integration of intelligence, technology, and human expertise.

Origin

Belfast, 2009

Kairon Technology was established in Belfast, Northern Ireland, by a group of former military intelligence officers and private sector security professionals. The founding team identified a critical gap between corporate security needs and the sophisticated, intelligence-led approaches employed by state actors.

Over fifteen years, Kairon has grown from a regional consultancy into an international firm with operational presence in 17 countries, serving government ministries, multinational corporations, financial institutions, and critical infrastructure operators.

Our History

Corporate Timeline

2009
Foundation in Belfast

Kairon Technology is established by former MI5 and SAS officers to provide intelligence-led security consultancy to private-sector clients.

2011
First Government Contract

Awarded a classified infrastructure protection contract by a European government ministry, marking Kairon's entry into the sovereign client sector.

2014
Cyber Division Established

The Cipher Division is formed to address the growing convergence of physical and digital threats facing Kairon's expanding client base.

2016
Middle East Expansion

Regional office opened in Dubai. Kairon extends operations into the Gulf Cooperation Council, providing executive protection and facility security across five countries.

2019
Asia-Pacific Office — Singapore

A Singapore regional hub is established to serve financial institutions and technology corporations operating across Southeast Asia and the Pacific Rim.

2022
250th Asset Protected

Kairon reaches the milestone of 250 protected assets globally, cementing its position as a premier international security provider.

2024
Psychological Security Unit

A dedicated Psychological Security division is established, addressing human-vector vulnerabilities and insider threats as the threat landscape evolves.
