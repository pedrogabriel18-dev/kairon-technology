About the Division

Digital Supremacy

The Cipher Division operates Kairon's digital intelligence and cyber security capabilities from a hardened facility within the Belfast headquarters. The team comprises former GCHQ and NSA signals intelligence analysts, offensive security researchers, and digital forensics specialists.

Unlike conventional cybersecurity firms, Cipher does not merely defend — it pursues adversaries, understands their methodologies, and disrupts their operations. The division operates an active offensive intelligence capability that has led to the degradation of multiple state-aligned threat actors.

Threat Intelligence

Production of strategic and tactical intelligence assessments on nation-state and organised criminal cyber threats.

Digital Forensics

Post-incident analysis and evidence preservation to legal evidentiary standards for civil and criminal proceedings.

Red Teaming

Adversarial simulation of sophisticated threat actors against client infrastructure to identify genuine vulnerabilities.

SIGINT Support

Signals intelligence collection and analysis in support of Kairon's physical operations and client advisory mandates.

Dark Web Monitoring

Persistent monitoring of criminal and adversarial forums for client-specific intelligence and early warning indicators.

Incident Response

24/7 rapid response capability for active cyber incidents, from initial triage through to full remediation and hardening.
