Process

How to Apply

01
Submit Application

Send your CV and a covering letter to careers@kairon-technology.com citing the role reference in the subject line.

02
Initial Screening

Successful applicants are contacted within 14 working days for a confidential telephone screening with a member of the HR team.

03
Assessment

Shortlisted candidates attend a structured assessment including competency interviews, role-specific evaluations, and psychometric testing.

04
Offer & Onboarding

Successful candidates receive a formal offer conditional upon completion of security clearance checks and pre-employment verification.

05
Security Clearance

All Kairon roles require a minimum of SC-level security clearance. Vanguard and Phantom roles require DV clearance. Kairon sponsors clearance where eligible.

06
Final Deployment

New operatives complete a structured induction programme before first deployment, including unit-specific training and a supervised operational period.

CIPHER DIVISION — BELFAST

Tactical Analyst

Full-Time SC Clearance Required REF: KT-CA-041
Role Overview

The Tactical Analyst will support the Cipher Division's operational intelligence function, synthesising multi-source intelligence streams to produce actionable assessments for active Kairon deployments and client advisory mandates.

Key Responsibilities
Production of written intelligence assessments and threat reports for client briefings
Analysis of open-source, signals, and human intelligence inputs
Support to operational planning through pre-deployment environmental analysis
Maintenance of threat actor profiles and adversarial capability databases
Contribution to the 24/7 command centre monitoring function on a rotational basis
Requirements
Minimum five years of experience in a government, military, or private sector intelligence role
Demonstrable analytical writing skills to a professional intelligence standard
Current or eligible for SC-level security clearance
Proficiency in a second language is advantageous
