Sentinel Unit

Physical Security & Site Protection

About the Unit

Our Foundation

The Sentinel Unit forms the physical backbone of Kairon's operational capacity. Staffed by former British Army, Royal Marines, and international special operations personnel, Sentinel operatives are trained to the highest standards of physical protection, tactical response, and crisis management.

Established at Kairon's founding in 2009, the Sentinel Unit has evolved from a small team of eight into an internationally deployed force of over 80 active operatives, with specialist sub-teams for maritime, aviation, and critical infrastructure environments.

Mission Statement

To Provide an Unbreachable Physical Shield Around Every Asset Entrusted to Kairon.

The Sentinel Unit operates on the principle that effective physical security is not passive. Every deployment is approached as an active intelligence-supported operation, where guards are not merely observers but trained responders capable of escalating to full tactical response within seconds.

Capabilities

Core Competencies

Static Protection

24/7 armed and unarmed guarding for permanent and temporary deployments across all facility types and threat levels.

Mobile Patrol

Randomised, intelligence-informed patrol patterns preventing hostile actors from profiling security routines.

Access Control

Design, installation, and management of layered access control systems integrating biometric and credential-based technologies.

Crisis Response

Immediate escalation capability from deterrence to active response, with standing procedures for all foreseeable incident types.

Hostile Environment

Specialist teams certified for operations in conflict-adjacent zones, including maritime and remote site deployments.

Medical Response

All Sentinel operatives hold current emergency first aid at work certification, with senior operatives trained to trauma care standard.
