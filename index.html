OP — 0041

Operation Sentinel

Active
Overview

Operation Sentinel is an ongoing multi-jurisdictional security engagement protecting a critical energy infrastructure complex spanning three sovereign territories in the North Sea region. Kairon was contracted following a series of unexplained sabotage incidents attributed to state-aligned actors.

Mandate

Continuous perimeter intelligence, physical deterrence, and cyber-physical integration across offshore platforms and onshore processing facilities. The operation encompasses 24/7 monitoring, rapid-response positioning, and counterintelligence support for facility management.

Methodology

Kairon deployed a rotating Sentinel Unit of 14 operatives supported by the Cipher Division's remote monitoring infrastructure. Drone surveillance networks, acoustic sensors, and behavioral analytics platforms were integrated into a unified command picture.

Operational Status

Active. Contract extended through Q4 2026. Zero security incidents since deployment. Client satisfaction assessed as exceptional across all performance metrics.

OP — 0033

Project Atlas

Completed
Overview

Project Atlas was a high-sensitivity executive protection and intelligence operation supporting a delegation of six principals during fourteen days of bilateral trade negotiations in an undisclosed Eastern European location. The engagement included pre-deployment threat assessment, route security, and signals monitoring.

Mandate

Provide seamless executive protection without disrupting the diplomatic atmosphere of the negotiations. Maintain a full intelligence picture of the local threat environment and advise principals on risk-adjusted movement decisions in real time.

Result

All principals were safely extracted at the conclusion of negotiations. Intelligence gathered during the operation was subsequently shared with the client's internal security directorate, resulting in the identification of two hostile surveillance operations targeting the delegation.
