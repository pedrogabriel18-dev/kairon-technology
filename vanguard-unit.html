About the Unit

Tier-One Capability

Vanguard is Kairon's highest-readiness operational unit, composed exclusively of former tier-one special forces operators and intelligence officers. All Vanguard operatives have served in elite military units including SAS, SBS, Delta Force, or equivalent international formations.

The unit maintains a global deployment readiness of 72 hours, supported by a pre-positioned logistics network in key strategic regions. No other private security provider can match Vanguard's combination of individual operator quality and organisational deployment speed.

Close Protection

Personal protection details for heads of state, senior executives, and VIP principals operating in all threat environments.

Hostile Extraction

Rapid extraction of principals from deteriorating or collapsed security environments, including active conflict zones.

Counter-Surveillance

Detection and disruption of hostile surveillance operations targeting Kairon's principals and clients.

Maritime Security

Protection of vessels, offshore assets, and maritime logistics chains operating in high-risk waters.

Aviation Security

Air transport protection for high-value cargo and principal movements on both commercial and private aviation.

Training & Capacity

Delivery of close protection and tactical training programmes to client internal security teams.
